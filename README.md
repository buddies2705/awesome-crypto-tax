**Awesome Crypto Tax** is a deeply-curated directory of every crypto tax tool worth knowing in 2026. Crypto tax is no longer "just sell, declare gains" — DeFi, NFTs, airdrops, staking, liquid staking, LP positions, MEV rewards, and per-wallet cost basis (now mandatory in the US) all need specialised handling.

This list covers:

- 🟣 **Solana-specific tools** (Awaken, Netrunner, CoinLedger Jupiter, Stake.tax)
- 🎨 **NFT-specific tools** (CoinLedger, Awaken, Magic Eden / Tensor / OpenSea exports)
- 🏢 **Enterprise / institutional accounting** (Bitwave, Cryptio, Ledgible, Cryptoworth, Sovos / TaxBit Enterprise)

- 🔌 **Tax APIs** (CoinLedger, TaxBit, Koinly) for builders

- 👛 **Portfolio trackers** that double as tax-export sources (Step Finance, Zerion, DeBank, Zapper, CoinStats)
- 📋 **Country tax authority guidance** (IRS, HMRC, ATO, CRA, BMF, NTA, ITD)

### What changed for the 2025/26 tax year

- 🆕 **US: Per-wallet cost basis mandatory** as of Jan 1 2026 (no more universal pooling)
- 🆕 **Form 1099-DA** rolled out by US exchanges starting 2025
- 🆕 **MiCA reporting** in the EU now in force
- 🆕 **DeFi 1099 enforcement** delayed but coming
- 🆕 **DAOs and AI agents** complicate "income vs sale" classification
- 🆕 **AI tax tools** (Awaken AI, CoinLedger Auto-Reconcile) replace hours of manual cleanup
